FlyWay Chat

hiatus

It's my attempt at a real alternative to the IM oligopoly. What makes an alternative fall into this category? In my humble opinion, ease of migration. You can have the exact same features and UI as one of the big ones. You may be even better than them. But if you can't get your users to bring over their friends with them... well, they won't use your product.

Communication is a social skill, and thus it requires agreement. You can't just switch platforms and force everyone else to do so as well. That's why these platforms become big monopolies. What's the point of switching platform, if nobody else does?

But I think I've got the secret sauce that'd allow my idea to work, and that's why I'd like to revive this project!

See it on GitHub
